# TabTuck privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

TabTuck saves the browser tabs you choose and restores them later. Everything it saves stays in your browser on your device. TabTuck has no account, no server, no analytics, and no ads, and it does not send your data anywhere.

## What TabTuck stores

When you save tabs, TabTuck stores:

- The address (URL) and title of each tab you save, and the time you saved it.
- How you organize them: collection names, folders and their icons, stars, and locks.
- A short undo history of the tabs you recently deleted, so you can restore them.
- Your options, such as the light or dark theme.

TabTuck reads the address and title of open tabs only when you save them, with the toolbar page, the right-click menu, or the keyboard shortcut.

## Where it is stored

All of this is kept in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. TabTuck makes no network requests. Site icons in the list come from your browser's own icon cache, not from an outside service. The fonts are included in the extension.

## Who can see it

Only you. TabTuck does not sell, share, or transfer your data, and does not use it for anything except saving and restoring your tabs. Nobody, including the developer, can see what you save.

Your data leaves the extension only when you do it yourself: when you copy links to the clipboard, or export a backup file to your device.

## How to delete it

- Delete single tabs or whole collections in TabTuck.
- Remove the extension from your browser to delete everything it stored.

## Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

If TabTuck ever changes how it handles your data, this page and the extension will say so before the change takes effect.
